Kaoda is one of the sons of a river lord of Vehmas. Once married to Maeve, he will become the king of Vehmas, though his title is merely nominal.
He is calm and reserved, and enjoys his solitude. He is passionate about the violin.

Childhood

Kaoda grew up in the Vehmas rivers. He was never very well looked after, although he was one of the sons of a powerful lord.

Maeve's reign

Although the idea of ​​marrying a stranger or becoming king didn't initially appeal to him, Kaoda eventually found a trusted ally in Maeve.
Both appreciated peace and quiet and had few friends, so they naturally became close, able to spend long periods together without exchanging a word, simply enjoying each other's company. While love wasn't present in their marriage, a strong friendship bound them.

As king, he had no more power than Maeve. Their role was only to appear publicly at parties and events.

Destruction of Vehmas

When Kaoda became aware of the charade of the world he lived in and the fate awaiting the person closest to him, he gradually descended into madness.
He became paranoid, convinced that the entire world was a threat. In a fit of madness, he set fire to the Mother Tree, destroying the Vehmas Forest, its inhabitants, and unleashing monsters upon the world. Without water and the forest's magic, he too perished.
