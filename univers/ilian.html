Ilian grew up in Painari where people thought the Khānbs were fallen gods. They revere one but are afraid to come across one. He was therefore always rejected and spent years on the streets before being taken in by Mercure. He therefore hates the inhabitants of the city and their view of the world.
He is extremely angry and has a great hatred in him. He is fully committed to everything he does and doesn’t hesitate to sacrifice what he loves or people if it allows him to accomplish his goal.
He has the power to teleport a distance of a few feet and his body is more muscular than a human. His young age and his strength make him particularly powerful but he has great difficulty in controlling himself.

Childhood

26 March 1256

Mercure found Ilian when he was still a baby in a dark alley in Painari. No one knows how he got there. Mercure raised him as best he could. They have always been very poor and did not always have enough to eat.
Ilian started stealing at a young age in order to have enough to survive.

Painarian Revolution

Ilian served as a detonator for the revolution. He was the first to attack the government head-on by destroying the Cathedral of the Sun. At the start of the revolution, he was ready to slaughter the Emperor's entire family until he met Stella. She was the first to have been able to calm her anger. After the fall of the emperor, he left with her in her exile far from the lands of Painari.

Ash War

After their exile, Ilian and Stella became mercenaries. During the war, they were dispatched by Gyulladt to find a mage capable of freeing the hero from his eternal ice.
