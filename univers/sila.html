Sila is a very gentle and calm person. She is cheerful and positive in nature.

She is very careful with her appearance. She loves pretty clothes, and often does hairstyles. She wears makeup and likes to draw things on her cheeks (hearts, stars, fake freckles)

Sila is a water magician, able to summon golems. Her powers are quite powerful and she is able to summon several at once if they are not too big.
Poc is the smallest of his golems and is summoned almost all the time. Gom is the tallest, and he serves as his watery armor for combat

Childhood

18 october 1249

Sila grew up in the countryside with both of her parents. Her family was very loving. When she was 16, she left to join Yunania to develop her magic there. She lived there until she was 19 before joining Dawan to pursue her dream.

Today

She lives in the capital. She owns an apartment and works in a small pastry shop. She dreams of owning her own brand. But she's not a very ambitious person, she loves her job and is happy with what she has.

Ash War

During the Ash War, Sila will be recruited by mercenaries to resurrect the Lost Hero to bring his powers to destroy monsters. Over time her powers have become very powerful and she knows how to defend herself very well. His water magic will be used to melt the eternal snows that imprisoned him. She will then take part in the fighting from a more strategic than violent point of view.
